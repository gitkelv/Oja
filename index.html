Ọjà

What building materials actually cost this week

Ask every dealer at once

Cement prices in Nigeria move within days, and the price lists online contradict each other by nearly double. Ọjà asks dealers directly, from your browser, and shows you what each one said — with the date they said it.

The quote board

Nothing asked yet. Each dealer you contact will appear here as its own card, with its own price and its own date. Ọjà will never merge them into a single figure.

Follow up

Ask about anything on the board — delivery windows, minimum orders, why two dealers disagree.

What is real here, and what is not

Working
The agent networks, the browser runtime, the cross-origin calls to each dealer, the consent step before every contact, and the arithmetic behind every total.
Representative
The three dealers are published by this project to demonstrate the protocol. They are fictional businesses in an invented town, Ilara Junction. Their prices are drawn from dated published Nigerian market reporting, cited on each dealer's own page.
Not real
This is not live dealer inventory and nothing here can be bought. No order you place goes anywhere.
No single price
Ọjà never merges dealer quotes into one figure. Published Nigerian cement prices already contradict each other by nearly double, and averaging contradictory sources is what makes those price lists useless — it turns a disagreement you could have acted on into a confident number that is wrong. Each dealer stays separate, with the date it quoted.
