What does cement actually cost today?

A bag runs ₦9,600 or ₦14,000 depending on who you ask and when. Ask once here, and every dealer answers for themselves.

Every dealer answers here on its own card — price, minimum order, delivery and the date it quoted. A run takes a couple of minutes: each dealer is a separate business being asked a real question.

What is real here, and what is not

Working
The agent networks, the browser runtime, the cross-origin calls to each dealer, the approval step before every contact, and the arithmetic behind every total.
Representative
The three dealers are published by this project to demonstrate the protocol. They are fictional businesses in an invented town, Ilara Junction. Their prices come from dated published Nigerian market reporting, cited on each dealer's own page.
Not real
This is not live dealer inventory, and nothing here can be bought.
No single price
Ọjà never merges quotes into one figure. Published Nigerian cement prices already contradict each other by nearly double, and averaging contradictory sources is what makes those lists useless: it turns a disagreement you could have acted on into a confident number that is wrong.
